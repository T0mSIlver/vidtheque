How the wall was built

One cover per talk: the highest-sharpness keyframe between 12% and 88% of the runtime, so no title cards and no end slates. Downscaled to 300px wide — the whole wall of 78 is under 1 MB. Every fourth talk also prefetches three more frames spread across its timeline; those are the ones that flip under the loupe.

What the numbers are

Counted once, live, off vidtheque.db at build time: videos where index_state='ready', and straight count(*) on cues, ocr_lines and keyframes. Word count is whitespace-split cues.text, so it is approximate and says so here rather than in a tooltip.

Speaker names are parsed out of the title — speakers is empty in this corpus.

Every frame on this wall is a builder's own work. vidtheque watched the talks and points back at them, to the second; the videos belong to the people who made them.
